Human review by design

Automation without giving up control.

IronSales automates internal sales administration while keeping humans involved in important decisions. Customer-facing emails and business commitments can remain reviewable by your sales team unless you explicitly approve another workflow.

AI can make mistakes. The system is designed around rules, validation, and appropriate review instead of pretending AI is perfect.

FAQ

Clear answers before you begin.

What counts as a processed sales call?

One sales-call transcript successfully submitted to and processed through the IronSales workflow.

Does IronSales automatically send follow-up emails?

Drafts can be generated automatically, but customer-facing emails can remain subject to human review depending on your configuration.

What CRMs can IronSales work with?

Integrations depend on your systems and approved workflow. We scope the right configuration during implementation.

Does IronSales replace sales reps?

No. IronSales removes repetitive administrative work so reps can focus more time on selling.

Is AI always accurate?

No AI system is perfect. IronSales uses workflow rules, validation, and human review to reduce errors.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Current plans have a $0 setup fee.
